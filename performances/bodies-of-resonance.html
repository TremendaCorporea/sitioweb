Bodies of Resonance

Sonic walk & ceramic / sound installation · summer 2021 · Werdersee, Bremen

An invitation to a sonic walk around the Werdersee, paired with a ceramic and sound installation. Growing out of the collective's early exploration process around the topic of resonance, the recorded sound material was later reactivated within the frame of a sound performance at aRaum, a temporary project space in Bremen.
