About us

Tremenda Corporea is a collective of interdisciplinary artists, working in the interplay of performance, technology, design and sonic exploration. Coming from different academic and cultural backgrounds — fine arts, design, dance — we explore collective methodologies of creation, where decisions are often the result of intense processes of experimentation and dialogue. Our process includes many ways of coming together: hybrid and in-presence sessions of writing scores, compositions, performances and situations of sharing.

Our collaboration is driven by the wish to dive into our relation to nature, the climate crisis and the human / non-human world. Our members come from Latin America and Europe and are currently based between Berlin and Bremen.

The collective plays with an almost non-intentional process of documenting, reflecting, performing, moving, volcanoing, reading, eating, creating. We use scores as fluid narrative structures that bring our bodies together. Since a score can be performed in infinite variations, we find ourselves in a process where our bodies naturally enjoy the partly-knowing — we almost never know with complete certainty the outcome of a score. We cherish this process of letting goals blur away.

History

Tremenda Corporea had its initial moment in March 2020, at the last event before Covid-19 made further events impossible: Victor, Icaro and Carla found out that they all shared the wish to play in an experimental music group. Icaro and Victor had applied to an open call at Schwankhalle Bremen; the rejection turned into an opportunity — instead of being selected and funded, Schwankhalle offered them rehearsal spaces and stages left unused because of the pandemic.

Together with Carla and Rahel, they undertook a collaborative exploration process circling around the topic of resonance. In summer 2021 they invited people to a sonic walk around the Werdersee and a ceramic / sound installation titled Bodies of Resonance. Later the recorded sound material was reactivated as a sound performance at aRaum, a temporary project space in Bremen — a former cellphone shop, now a chicken imbiss.

Collectively caring for a wild, diverse garden, we work on projects together and support each other on our individual paths.
