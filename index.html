A collective of interdisciplinary artists moving between performance, technology, design and sound.

Coming from fine arts, design and dance, and from Latin America and Europe, we explore collective methodologies of creation — scores as fluid narrative structures that bring our bodies together around our relation to nature, the climate crisis and the human / non-human world. Currently based between Berlin and Bremen.
